WirdWaqt Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026 · Applies to the WirdWaqt Android app (com.iamwaqargulzar.wirdwaqt)

In short
  • WirdWaqt has no account, no advertising and no analytics.
  • The Google Play version of WirdWaqt has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data to us or anyone else.
  • Your location, prayer records, Tasbih counts and settings are stored only on your device.

Who we are

WirdWaqt is an independent app developed by Waqar Gulzar. It provides prayer times, prayer alarms, a Tasbih counter, an Islamic and civil calendar and a Qibla compass.

Place-name lookup

After a GPS fix, WirdWaqt asks Android's built-in geocoding service for the name of your town so it can label your location. On many phones that system service is provided by Google and may send the coordinates to Google to find the name. That lookup is handled by your phone's operating system under its own privacy terms (for Google, the Google Privacy Policy). WirdWaqt does not receive, store or share anything from that lookup apart from the place name shown in the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, rent or share personal data.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not use your IP address to find your location.
  • We do not run servers that receive data from the app.

Backups

If Android device backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include WirdWaqt's settings, prayer records and Tasbih routines in your device backup to your Google account, encrypted and managed by Google, so they can be restored on a new phone. You can turn this off in Android's backup settings.

WirdWaqt also lets you export a backup file yourself under Settings → Backup & restore. The file goes only where you choose to save it.

Permissions

  • Location: prayer times and Qibla for your position (optional).
  • Notifications, exact alarms, foreground service and wake lock: to sound the Adhan and Jamaat alarms on time and let you stop them.
  • Run at startup: to restore your alarms after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for Tasbih and alarm feedback.

Children

WirdWaqt is suitable for general audiences and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

All app data lives on your device. Clearing WirdWaqt's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes it.
